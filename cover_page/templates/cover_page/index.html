A NOVEL

The Quiet
Between Stars

Somewhere, the sky remembers us.

When Mira returns to the island where she grew up, she finds the old observatory locked, the lighthouse dark, and a letter addressed to her in her mother's handwriting.

Its pages lead her across wind-worn cliffs and star-filled nights, toward a secret her family has kept for generations. But the closer Mira gets to the truth, the more she must decide what home means—and what she is willing to leave behind.

“A luminous story about memory, belonging, and the courage to begin again.” — The Reading Room

BY

ELENA MARLOWE

Elena writes stories about faraway places and the people who find their way back.
